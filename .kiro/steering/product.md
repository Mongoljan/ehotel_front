# Product Overview

GoTrip is a comprehensive travel and booking platform built with Next.js. It's a multi-service travel application that provides:

## Core Services
- **Hotels**: Hotel search, booking, and management
- **Flights**: Flight search and booking functionality  
- **Tours**: Tour packages and experiences
- **Car Rental**: Vehicle rental services
- **Activities**: Local activities and experiences
- **Cruises**: Cruise booking and information
- **Rentals**: Property rental services

## Key Features
- Multi-language support (English, Mongolian)
- Responsive design with mobile-first approach
- Real-time search and filtering
- User dashboard and booking management
- Payment integration (Qpay, bank transfer)
- E-receipt (e-barimt) generation
- Live chat support integration
- SEO-optimized pages

## Target Market
Travel booking platform focused on comprehensive travel services with strong internationalization support, particularly for English and Mongolian markets.

## Business Model
Commission-based booking platform with integrated payment processing and customer support systems.
